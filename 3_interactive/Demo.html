Correcting a near-sighted eye

Q) Glasses with power $P > -0.25$ D.  What is the min far point of the eye? $P = \dfrac1f$  ($f$ in m, $P$ in diopters)
Needed: $P = -1/d_{\text{far}}$ ≈  D
$\dfrac1f = \dfrac1{d_o} + \dfrac1{d_i}$,   $d_o = \infty$,   $d_i = -d_{\text{far}}$
$\Rightarrow\; P = \dfrac1f = -\dfrac{1}{d_{\text{far}}}$
$-\dfrac{1}{d_{\text{far}}} > -0.25 \;\Rightarrow\;$ $d_{\text{far}} > 4$ m

26.8 Lensmaker
The two surfaces of a lens have radii of $r_1 = -20$ cm and $r_2 = +5$ cm. The index of refraction of the lens is $n = 1.52$. What kind of lens is it?
$\dfrac1f = \left(\dfrac{n_{\text{glass}}}{n_{\text{air}}} - 1\right)\left(-\dfrac1{20} - \dfrac15\right) = 0.52\left(-\dfrac{5}{20}\right)$
$\Rightarrow\; f \approx -7.7$ cm $< 0$: diverging

Summary

  • Power $P = 1/f$ in diopters ($f$ in m); near-sighted eye: $P = -1/d_{\text{far}}$
  • Lens maker: $\dfrac1f = \left(\dfrac{n_{\text{lens}}}{n_{\text{medium}}} - 1\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{R_1} - \dfrac{1}{R_2}\right)$
  • $f > 0$ converging (thicker in the middle), $f < 0$ diverging (thinner in the middle)
  • Two in-phase sources: constructive if $\Delta d = m\lambda$, destructive if $\Delta d = (m+\frac12)\lambda$
